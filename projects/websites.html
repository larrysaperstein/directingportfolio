Why do you offer a One-Page Site?

If you're still building credits and materials, a streamlined website can be a smart option. It gives you:

  • A professional online presence
  • One easy link to share
  • A clean presentation of your materials
  • Room to expand later

Think of it as a professional step-up from a Linktree. Fully customizable and fully you. You can always upgrade your site when you’re ready.

What additional features do you offer?

Any site can be upgraded with additional pages and features, such as:

  • Multiple Galleries
  • Dedicated media pages
  • Contact forms
  • Press and reviews
  • Ticket sales/Tour dates integration
  • Embedding your podcast or Substack
  • Teaching and coaching information
  • News/Upcoming events
  • Advanced design customization

From beautiful landing pages to complex personal portfolios, I can create a solution that fits your goals and budget. The sky is the limit!

Why should I work with you and not Squarespace?

As an actor and artist myself, I specialize in websites for performers and understand what casting directors and industry professionals need to see.

I handle the design, setup, and launch, so you don’t have to deal with confusing website builders or monthly subscription fees.

Most DIY website platforms charge ongoing monthly fees. My sites are built with no required monthly website charges. Once the site is live, you only pay for your domain name, typically around $20 per year.

If you did the math, that means the website pays for itself over time.
